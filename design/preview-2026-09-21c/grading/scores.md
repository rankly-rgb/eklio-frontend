## 5. The three changes that would most raise the overall mean

**1. Fix the copy layer before anything is drawn.** Elaboration is the lowest criterion at 2.47 and every one of the 1s and most of the 2s trace to text, not to drawing: a truncated title (19), an ungrammatical one (02), three cards whose title is their own first field line (09/18/24), five duplicate-title pairs, a gloss promoted to a headline (29), and a contact card naming a different practitioner than its own footer (36). Reject any title that ends on a function word, any title that string-matches a field in the same card or a title elsewhere in the batch, and bind the practitioner name to one source. This alone moves elaboration on ~10 posts and costs no drawing.

**2. Make the field grid fit its text, and put a gloss in every field.** Legibility scored 2 on eight posts, always for the same reason: 280px columns forcing "The deadline", "Looking fine", "The processing", "Week three", "Still capable" to wrap mid-phrase, glosses spilling to three lines, and the label/gloss size ratio collapsing to 1.16–1.22:1 against the reference's 1.375:1 — on 25 it reaches 1:1. Either widen the columns to the reference's field widths or drop from three columns to two, set one fixed gloss size (no per-field auto-shrink as on 22), and give the four gloss-less cards (20, 23, 26, 29) their second line. This lifts legibility and elaboration together.

**3. One distinct illustration per card at reference scale, and open up the two collapsed tint steps.** Four posts share a pixel-identical door, two a pixel-identical ring set, two a pixel-identical thread, two a pixel-identical iceberg, and sixteen panels the same 224×9 squiggle; meanwhile 22's plant is a quarter of the reference's area and 14's anchor under half. Draw the object the card actually says — a sequence card should not get a loop (02, 16) — at 8–12% of the content band as the reference does. In parallel, move tint 1 off the paper (`#F4EEE3` → `#F0E7D8` or darker; ΔE76 3.2 → 6.5+) and replace the second tan `#D1AA73` with a fourth hue, as the reference does with its sage `#DCE0D3`, so that 11, 20 and 26 stop carrying two lightnesses of one colour.
