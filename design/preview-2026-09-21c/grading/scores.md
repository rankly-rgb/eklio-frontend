# Grading — 30 post visuals against the eleven reference compositions

Scale 1–5 on each of: **illustration** (a drawn object present, carrying the card's meaning), **tints** (own tint per part, softened flats), **legibility** (readable, clear hierarchy, no crowding or collision), **elaboration** (same level of work as the reference of that composition type).

The lone sentence and the comparison pair are scored against what they are specified to be (text plus a small drawn mark; tinted fields that are themselves speech bubbles), not against the stroked-object rule.

## 3. Named defects found

**Title stops mid-sentence / ends on a word needing the next**
- **14-concentric_control** — "Efficiency can mask what".
- **02-cycle** — "Productivity can be freeze" (not truncated, but ungrammatical in the same way: a dropped word).

**Two cards whose titles say the same thing**
- **20-concentric_control** "Competence masks exhaustion" and **24-annotated_curve** "Competence & exhaustion" — the same claim. **12-annotated_curve** "Competence and shutdown" is a third variant of it.
- **02-cycle** "Productivity can be freeze" and **22-single_statement** "Productivity can be a trauma response masquerading as competence" — the same claim.
- **07** and **28** — the same claim in different words.

**Title is simply its own field labels repeated**
- **04**, **13**, **19** (all three practitioner cards) — the title is the first line of the tinted field, word for word.
- **24-annotated_curve** — the title is the two axis labels joined by an ampersand.

**Text overlapping another element** — none found. The narrow-column cards (05, 14, 20, 26) come very close but no glyph actually collides.

**Additional defects not on the checklist**
- Both carousels (**03**, **27**) are single cover slides; the composition type is never produced.
- Six cards carry **no gloss in any field** (15, 18, 21, 24, and partially 08) — the fields are then bare labels in half-empty boxes.
- **21** repeats a tint across two of four fields, breaking "each part gets its own tint".
- Only **six distinct drawings** serve twenty-one diagram cards, and each is byte-identical across its type (door ×3, rings ×4, head ×3, anchor ×2, plant ×3, curve ×2, thread ×2). The illustration is a per-type constant, never keyed to what the card says.
- The **surface-and-beneath** composition — one of the eleven references — is never used.
- **Zero dark-ground cards in thirty.** Within spec (≤3 in 12), but the whole set is one paper tone, which is part of why it reads flat.
- Title:label ratio sits around **1.7–2.0×** on most diagram cards — at or just under the specified 2× floor.

## 4. The five weakest visuals

1. **03-carousel (10/20)** — a six-panel carousel delivered as one cover slide. Nothing of the composition type exists: no panels, no tints, no illustration. It is indistinguishable from 01.
2. **27-carousel (10/20)** — the same failure a second time, at 1/5.
3. **08-comparison_pair (12/20)** — the speech-bubble silhouette is the whole illustration for this type, and it is applied to only two of four fields; the cream tail is nearly invisible on cream paper. Labels wrap into orphan words and the top-left bubble is a third empty.
4. **21-numbered_strategies (12/20)** — four bare labels, no glosses anywhere, and a repeated tint so two of four fields read as the same colour. The plant does not carry the timeline the labels describe.
5. **24-annotated_curve (13/20)** — title is its own axis labels repeated, no glosses, an unannotated curve identical to 12's, and the same claim as 20. Tied with **12-annotated_curve (13/20)**, whose curve is equally unannotated.

Worth naming alongside these: **14-concentric_control**, whose truncated title ("…mask what") is the single clearest textual defect in the set, and the **04/13/19** practitioner trio, which are three copies of one card with the title echoing the field.

## 5. The three changes that would most raise the overall mean

**1. Give every tinted field a gloss, and never let a title repeat a field.** (Lifts elaboration — the weakest criterion at 3.07 — on 15, 18, 21, 24, 04, 13, 19: seven cards, most of them currently at 2.) Six cards ship as bare labels in half-empty boxes, and four titles are their own field text read back. Requiring label + gloss in every field, and forbidding a title that is a substring of its own content, converts the thinnest third of the set into cards that look like the references. Highest return of the three.

**2. Actually produce the carousel: three or more populated panels, not a cover slide.** (Lifts 03 and 27 from 1 to 3–4 on elaboration and from 2 to 4 on illustration and tints.) Two of thirty cards currently deliver one-sixth of their composition. Fixing them also removes two of the eleven near-identical lone-sentence layouts from the set, which is itself a density gain.

**3. Fix the text fitter: no title may break mid-phrase, and no field may be narrower than its longest label.** (Lifts legibility on 14, 20, 05, 26, 12, 08 — the six cards below 4.) The three-column cards force two-line labels and three-line glosses that collapse the label/gloss hierarchy, and 14's title is cut off at "what". Reflowing to two columns, or shortening the labels to fit one line, removes the only real readability failures in the set.

*Beyond these three: the six reused per-type drawings and the absence of any dark-ground card are what keep the set feeling uniform even where each card is individually correct.*
